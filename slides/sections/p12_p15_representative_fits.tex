            w'_y=\mathrm{Reweight}\times
            \frac{N_y^{\mathrm{DATA}}}{\sum_i w_{y,i}}.
          \]
        \item Merge 2023 and 2024 MC and DATA; fit one signal yield, shape and background.
        \item The $N_{23}^{\mathrm{DATA}}:N_{24}^{\mathrm{DATA}}$ mixture is descriptive, not a physical signal-yield ratio.
      \end{itemize}
